    DESCRIBE fullstack

    Welcome to the future of development. In the age of AI, specialization alone is not enough. Backend developers must understand the frontend. Frontend developers must master databases. Mobile developers must think cloud.

    Full stack is not a single stack — it’s the ability to combine technologies, adapt, and deliver complete solutions. Whether you’re mixing Flutter with Java, Kotlin with SQL, or React with PHP, you are building the future.

    This conference is here to help you extend your knowledge, break boundaries, and become superheroes with full stack powers.
